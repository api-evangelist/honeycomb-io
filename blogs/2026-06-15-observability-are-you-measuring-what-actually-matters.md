Old observability metrics like uptime and MTTR aren't enough anymore; teams must connect technical signals to business outcomes, especially as AI raises the stakes. The piece argues that "slow is the new down" and that organizations should measure across five dimensions—savings, stability, speed, satisfaction, and product success—tying technical improvements to outcomes customers and stakeholders care about.
